A poem to the love of my Life❤️

happy valentine’s day my love💗 i don’t even know where to start because sometimes words feel too small for how i feel about you. you’ve become such an important part of my life in a way i didn’t even expect. being with you feels natural, calm, and real, like i don’t have to try to be anyone else but myself. i love the way you care, the way you think, the way you show up for me even in the smallest moments. it’s not just the big things… it’s the little conversations, the random laughs, the way you make my days softer just by being in them. you mean more to me than i say sometimes. and even when i don’t always get my words right, my heart is always with you. i want to keep learning you, growing with you, and choosing you over and over again. thank you for loving me, for being patient with me, and for being the person i can call mine. i’m really grateful for you. happy valentine’s day, my girl. i love you 💗
